From Strategy to Sustainability Impact

Membumi helps turn those initiatives into sustainability impacts that actually hold up. By combining certified methodology and experienced people, our work becomes a clear measure of your governance, social, environmental, and economic commitments.

Our Services


Sustainability Report

Simplifying sustainability compliance using GRI-aligned reporting, guided by POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 and No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021.

Social Mapping

Assessing community needs, local potentials, and challenges to align sustainability initiatives with PERMENLHK No. 7 of 2025.

Stakeholder Engagement

Facilitating stakeholder engagement through the AA1000 Stakeholder Engagement Standard and aligned with PERMENLHK No. 7 of 2025.

SROI Analysis

Measuring social impact to understand value created for communities using the Social Value International Standard, aligned with PERMENLHK No. 7 of 2025.
